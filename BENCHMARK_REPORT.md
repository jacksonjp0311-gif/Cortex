## Executive boundary

Cortex has verified mechanisms for bounded context, canonical provenance,
provider-neutral model circulation, independent evaluation, governance, and
controlled competence experiments. One real provider-backed model boundary was
crossed before v9.8.

The first live A–E competence cohort produced success in every arm (`n=1`) and
therefore zero measured lift. Cortex has **not** demonstrated that distilled
competence improves a model. v9.8 preserves that null and adds semantic-support
and preregistered causal-evidence gates for the next experiment.

## v9.8 measurement changes

- New competence semantic identities bind operational material beside IDs.
- Immutable semantic witnesses distinguish exact canonical support from
  unsupported/unknown generalization.
- Confirmatory trials require a frozen pre-execution design.
- Primary binary effects use exact matched-pair discordance tests with Holm
  multiplicity correction.
- A one-case panel reports variance as not estimable.
- Fixture evidence remains structural and cannot satisfy empirical promotion.
