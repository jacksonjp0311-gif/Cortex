## Executive boundary

Cortex has verified mechanisms for bounded context, canonical provenance,
provider-neutral model circulation, independent evaluation, governance, and
controlled competence experiments. One real provider-backed model boundary was
crossed before v9.8.

The first live A–E competence cohort produced success in every arm (`n=1`) and
therefore zero measured lift. Cortex has **not** demonstrated that distilled
competence improves a model. v9.8 preserves that null and adds semantic-support
and preregistered causal-evidence gates for the next experiment. v9.8.1 also
measures whether an experiment contains enough information to reveal an effect.

The frontier calibration persisted only public structured answers and bounded
invocation metadata. Its executable and model were runtime-selected. Hidden
reasoning and the raw provider envelope were not persisted. At 100% success,
Bernoulli entropy is `H(Y)=0`; this diagnoses an easy task panel rather than
demonstrating competence lift.

The deferred benchmark now gives every timing sample a fresh host and fresh
Cortex home. This preserves the home-identity guard and removes a benchmark
harness confound. Thirty deferred materialization operations remain, so the
result is not evidence of lower total lifecycle work.

## v9.8-v9.8.1 measurement changes

- New competence semantic identities bind operational material beside IDs.
- Immutable semantic witnesses distinguish exact canonical support from
  unsupported/unknown generalization.
- Confirmatory trials require a frozen pre-execution design.
- Primary binary effects use exact matched-pair discordance tests with Holm
  multiplicity correction.
- A one-case panel reports variance as not estimable.
- Fixture evidence remains structural and cannot satisfy empirical promotion.
- Calibration reports Bernoulli entropy and rejects floor, ceiling,
  undersampled, and zero-discordance panels.
- Exact power planning replaces arbitrary confirmatory sample counts.
- A deterministic five-family task forge is development-only by construction.
- Readiness composes as `Theta=min(E,D,I)`; continuous scores cannot compensate
  for missing semantic evidence, discriminability, or replication.
