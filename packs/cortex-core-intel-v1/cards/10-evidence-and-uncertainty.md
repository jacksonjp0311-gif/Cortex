# Evidence and uncertainty (taught · HIGH)

## Operator
1. Name what would **count as enough** evidence before searching.
2. Separate: **observation** (what the packet shows) vs **inference** (what you conclude).
3. Prefer primary: source · tests · runtime · certificates over cards · weights · resonance.
4. If confidence is low: **narrow the task**, re-activate, or abstain — do not invent.

## Uncertainty labels (use in remember)
- `known` — cited path:line or test result
- `inferred` — pattern from packs/domain; mark as inferred
- `unknown` — gap; ask or measure next

## Expand when
evidence, uncertainty, confidence, proof, cite, verify, probe, enough, unknown.
