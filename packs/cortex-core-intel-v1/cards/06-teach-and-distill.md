# Teach and distill (taught intelligence)

## What teaching means here
Distill **methods and domain shape** into pack cards + CORTEXBF1 routing —
not copy an LLM's weights into binary.

## Distill loop
1. Observe what worked (fix, ritual, evolve verdict).
2. Write a short operator or invariant on a card.
3. `cortex packs install` (rebuild binary) → `packs index` → activate to verify expand.
4. Optional: `cortex distill` / `teach --seed` to seal doctrine into Discovery Cards.

## Teach seed interconnection
Teaching folds into the body: interconnect docs + pack cards + stream frames.
Agent should prefer lean glyph line + expanded pack cards over re-deriving doctrine.
