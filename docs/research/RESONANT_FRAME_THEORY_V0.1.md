# Resonant Frame Theory v0.1

**Status:** Experimental engineering theory for Cortex v7.3.0  
**Claim boundary:** Software telemetry and advisory policy only.

## 1. Status and claim boundary

Resonant Frames measure **bounded temporal coordination** across typed Cortex channels. They do **not** establish consciousness, biological equivalence, electromagnetic fields, quantum behavior, correctness, authority, witness, or host mutation permission.

Host source, tests, runtime evidence, body epoch, delegated capability, constitutional path, and independent witness remain controlling.

## 9. Integration versus differentiation

High integration with low differentiation = **overbound**. High integration with high differentiation and evidence alignment = **coherent differentiated**. Low integration = **fragmented**. High synchrony is **not** automatically healthy.

## 10. Overbinding and stale echo

**OVERBOUND:** coordinated collapse (common-mode domination).  
**STALE_ECHO:** memory-dominant coordination without adequate verified evidence or comparator quality.
