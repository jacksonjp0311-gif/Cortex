# Reading Cortex evidence

A claim is only as strong as its weakest required evidence boundary. Start with
[STATUS](STATUS.md), then follow its exact artifact and implementation verifier.
The [benchmark manifest](../benchmarks/results/MANIFEST.json) indexes files; its
hashes do not independently prove scientific validity.

| Evidence type | Can establish | Cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| Live model inference | External inference observed through the declared adapter | Provider attestation, Cortex benefit or general competence |
| Zero-call audit | Local deterministic behavior under sampled controls | New model performance or independent replication |
| Prospective experiment | A frozen protocol preceded observations | Correct instruments or causal validity automatically |
| Post-hoc analysis | A later bounded interpretation of existing observations | New inference, preregistration or fresh sample count |
| Development screening | Whether this small panel shows potential headroom | Population calibration or treatment effect |
| Fresh confirmation | More samples under a fixed prospective rule | Independence of semantically cloned tasks |
| Replication | Repeated effect under declared fresh conditions | Universality across tasks/models |
| Instrument assurance | Sampled valid alternatives accepted; broken controls rejected | Exhaustive semantic correctness |
| Transduction assurance | Declared transformations preserve intended artifacts | Trust in untested channels or the OS |
| Immutable receipt | Content and relationships reconstruct canonically | Truth, authority or experiment success |

## Review a claim

1. Resolve the exact source, task, model, evaluator, policy and artifact identities.
2. Separate record integrity from instrument and complete delivery-path validity.
3. Check chronology, exclusions, resource matching, dependence and current challenges.
4. Check which hypothesis was actually tested. Task-only accuracy is not treatment gain.
5. Keep unsupported prerequisites UNKNOWN. No later PASS compensates for failure.

The A/B/C hypothesis remains task-only versus irrelevant sham versus relevant
independent lesson. Relevance gain is `U_C - U_B`, not extra context volume.
Cumulative organization additionally requires retained evidence to change fresh
variation and improve matched outcomes under replication. Neither is established.

Evidence classes and domain verifiers live in [the system map](SYSTEM_MAP.md).
No evidence class authorizes host mutation or automatically admits durable memory.
