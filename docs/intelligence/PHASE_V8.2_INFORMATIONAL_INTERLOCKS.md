# Phase v8.2 — Informational Interlocks

## Purpose

v8.2 gives Cortex a measured answer to a previously unmeasured question: did a grounded evidence path and a learned route jointly contribute information about an independently verified outcome?

The field is functional telemetry. It is not consciousness, subjective sensing, a capability grant, or evidence that internal association outranks host source and tests.

## Typed field

The principal interlock is:

```text
E (grounded evidence) → L (learned route) → O (verified outcome) → E
```

An activation appends the unresolved E-L legs after its final body epoch is sealed. `record_outcome` closes O only after an independent verification type exists. Every row retains its exact body epoch. Analysis may span adaptive successor epochs only inside one measurement cohort: repository identity, evidence root, schema, and constitutional configuration must all match. This makes longitudinal learning measurable without mixing incompatible source or constitutional worlds. The ledger retains at most 4,096 observations per repository and stores paths in bounded sets.

The ledger is not another memory database. `neural_activations` remains route truth; `task_outcomes` remains outcome truth; `information_interlock_observations` is the receipt-bearing join between them.

## Math

For binary presence variables E and L and signed outcome O, v8.2 reports:

\[
S = \max\left(0, I(E,L;O)-\max(I(E;O), I(L;O))\right)
\]

This is a deliberately conservative synergy proxy, not a unique partial-information decomposition. Jeffreys smoothing stabilizes sparse contingency tables. Duplicate predictors produce redundancy and XOR produces joint-only information.

The bounded triadic alignment is:

\[
A_\tau = G_\tau\,(C_\tau S_\tau V_\tau)^{1/3}\,B_\tau\,(1-D_\tau)\,R_n
\]

where:

- \(G\) is the hard measurement-cohort constitutional/witness gate; exact epochs remain auditable;
- \(C\) is typed E-L-O closure over observed opportunities;
- \(S\) is synergy normalized by outcome entropy;
- \(V\) is independently valid outcome coverage;
- \(B\) is normalized balance entropy across closure, synergy, and validity;
- \(D\) combines redundancy and hub concentration penalties;
- \(R_n\) is bounded sample reliability, reaching one at 32 valid outcomes.

If the constitutional gate is false, alignment is exactly zero.

## Geometry discipline

Historical triadic telemetry used a top-degree projection capped at 400 nodes. That view is useful for bounded online enrichment but can substantially overstate closure in a dense-core/sparse-periphery graph. `cortex interlock geometry` now computes both:

- the complete relation-agnostic neural graph, which is the release reference;
- the capped top-degree projection, labeled as a projection.

The audit reports node coverage, triangle concentration, global-closure delta, mean-local ratio, and agreement at ±0.02. A failed agreement gate forbids treating the projection as whole-graph geometry.

## Noninterference and promotion

v8.2 is shadow-first:

- interlock values are outside the coherence component weights;
- cached route annotations are added after ranking and cannot change score or order;
- structure invention can require interlock evidence only through an explicit opt-in argument;
- no signal automatically promotes into policy or authority.

Promotion requires a future release receipt showing at least 32 valid samples per task family and 128 overall, outcome variation, one compatible measurement cohort with exact epoch receipts, complete witness coverage, positive lower lesion interval, sampling agreement, recall loss no worse than 2 percentage points, MRR regression no worse than 0.01, p95 latency increase no worse than 10%, and bounded storage. v8.2 reports recall and latency as unmeasured until an evaluation receipt supplies them.

## Operator surfaces

```bash
python -m cortex interlock status --repo MyProject --json

```

MCP exposes the same read-only field as `cortex_interlock`. Interconnect and coherence include compact panels. Coherence also emits an `observation_basis.comparable_key`; scores from different Governor or confidence contexts must not be compared as if they were the same measurement.

## Falsification suite

The phase is rejected or held in shadow if any of these fail:

1. XOR does not show joint-only information.
2. Duplicate predictors show positive synergy.
3. Missing constitutional or witness validity yields nonzero alignment.
4. Observations with different evidence, schema, or constitutional cohort enter one active field.
5. Route stamping changes ranking score or order.
6. Required interlock support permits unsupported topology invention.
7. Capped geometry is presented without its complete-graph bias audit.
8. Removing the synergy/outcome leg has no positive, confidence-bounded effect once data are ready.

## Claim boundary

The field detects a measurable statistical organization across evidence, learned routing, and verified outcomes. It may become useful for routing efficiency after validation. It does not establish experience, selfhood, sentience, autonomous intent, or permission to mutate a host repository.
