# Cortex v9.8.7 — Semantic Distillation Seal & Partial Evidence Calibration

## Purpose

Close the operational seam between valid trajectory lineage and supported
competence meaning, then measure the evidence-support transition exposed by
the v9.8.6 architecture floor/ceiling bracket.

## Distillation wound

The v9.8 witness could reconstruct exact public support, but ordinary transfer
and distribution did not require it. A lineage-valid candidate could therefore
carry an unsupported abstraction into the structural trial path and, for live
origins, approach empirical classification.

## Implemented seal

New competence candidates receive a separate immutable v1.1 distillation
witness. The witness ignores labels and explanatory prose, decomposes
operational fields into atomic public claims, and reconstructs exact support
from canonical evaluation and outcome receipts. It requires meaningful claims
on both `capability` and `intended_outcome`; identifiers alone cannot pass.

The candidate does not sign or mutate this proof. The proof is resolved and
reverified independently when a transfer trial is created and verified.

```text
candidate
  → canonical origin trajectory
  → public evaluation/outcome atoms
  → immutable distillation witness
  → tri-state semantic support
  → transfer gate
```

For a live or attested origin, transfer requires semantic support `pass`.
Synthetic/simulated trials may retain `unknown` solely for structural testing.
Production distribution requires `pass`; sandbox projection remains visibly
synthetic, sandbox-only, and non-promotable.

## Mathematical law

Let `L` be canonical lineage validity and `D` the independently reconstructed
distillation state under `FAIL < UNKNOWN < PASS`.

```text
Θ_distill = min(L, D)
EmpiricalTransfer(K) ⇒ Θ_distill = PASS
```

Continuous similarity, utility, prose fluency, model identity, and caller
assertions cannot compensate for `D != PASS`.

## Partial evidence intervention

For each architecture case, Cortex deterministically finds the
lexicographically first minimum coordinate subset that separates every latent
hypothesis. A development policy discloses a committed fraction of that set and
binds:

- the chosen indices;
- the disclosed candidate signature table;
- a commitment to undisclosed signatures;
- a commitment to the complete signature surface;
- the structural evidence geometry.

Raw observed schedules remain in the task. The intervention organizes evidence
rather than removing it, and never discloses the exact answer.

## Executed results

Two fresh four-case screens ran through the canonical external-model
circulation path using runtime-selected `grok-4.6` through the provider-neutral
JSON subprocess adapter.

| Declared fraction | Effective disclosed coordinates | Result | Median latency | Median tokens |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.50 | 1 | 4/4 | 39.49 s | 18,259.5 |
| 0.25 | 1 | 4/4 | 53.05 s | 19,045.5 |

The fractions collapsed to the same atomic intervention because screened
minimum resolving sets contained one or two coordinates and disclosure rounds
up to one. Together with the earlier `0/4` no-table and `4/4` full-table panels,
the evidence shows a zero-to-one representation discontinuity. It does not
locate a 30–70% calibration band.

Cross-case latency dispersion is observational and is not repeat-run variance.

## Tests and integrity

The adversarial surface covers identifier-only abstractions, unsupported
generalization, immutable witness recomputation, structural-only synthetic
trials, production semantic gating, read-only projection purity, prior corpus
hash preservation, deterministic disclosure, answer exclusion, and bounded
fractions.

## Claim boundary

v9.8.7 establishes that semantic support now gates empirical use and that one
organized evidence coordinate was sufficient for the screened architecture
cases. It does not establish general semantic entailment, completeness of
prerequisites/counterevidence, positive competence transfer, confirmatory
causal benefit, cognition, consciousness, agency, or authority.

## Next measurement

Freeze the same raw observations and vary representation granularity below one
complete coordinate: disclose the coordinate for a controlled subset of
candidate rows, or present an unaligned/shuffled/sham table with identical
token volume. This can distinguish semantic alignment from generic formatting
or context-volume effects.
